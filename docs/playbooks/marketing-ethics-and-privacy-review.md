# Marketing Ethics and Privacy Review

## Review areas
- Clear purpose and audience benefit
- Lawful basis, consent, and data minimization
- Sensitive audience and vulnerable-group considerations
- Transparent claims, pricing, and endorsements
- Fair targeting and exclusion risks
- Accessibility and inclusive representation
- Appropriate retention, access, and deletion
- Supplier and platform data handling
