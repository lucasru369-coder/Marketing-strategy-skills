# Meeting and Decision Rules

## Before
Share the decision needed, context, options, and pre-read. Invite only people needed to decide or contribute.

## During
Separate facts, assumptions, and preferences. Make trade-offs explicit. Capture dissent when it affects risk.

## After
Record decision, rationale, accountable role, due date, and review trigger. Distribute actions promptly.

If no decision is required, consider an asynchronous update.
