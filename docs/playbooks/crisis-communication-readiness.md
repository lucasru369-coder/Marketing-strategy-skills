# Crisis Communication Readiness

## Preparation
Define incident categories, severity levels, decision authority, spokesperson, legal review, employee guidance, and approved communication channels.

## Response sequence
1. Verify facts and identify what is unknown.
2. Protect affected people and stop harmful activity where possible.
3. Activate the response team.
4. Prepare a clear holding statement if needed.
5. Coordinate updates across channels.
6. Monitor response and correct inaccurate information.

## Incident log
| Time | Verified fact | Unknown | Action | Approver | Next update |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not speculate, assign blame prematurely, or disclose personal information. Prioritize accuracy, empathy, and timely correction.
