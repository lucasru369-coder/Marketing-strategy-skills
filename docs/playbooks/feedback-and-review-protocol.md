# Feedback and Review Protocol

## Before review
Confirm the version, review objective, decision owner, and feedback deadline. Ask reviewers to assess against the brief.

## Feedback format
Use location, issue, rationale, priority, and suggested direction. Separate factual corrections, strategic concerns, and personal preference.

## Resolution
Consolidate conflicting feedback through the accountable decision owner. Record accepted changes, rejected requests, and unresolved questions.

## Review log
| Version | Reviewer | Feedback | Category | Decision | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Avoid parallel contradictory instructions and untracked edits. Agree the number and purpose of review rounds at kickoff.
