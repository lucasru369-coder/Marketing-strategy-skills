# Retention Marketing

## Diagnose retention
Define the retained behavior, cohort, time window, and customer lifecycle. Distinguish repeat purchase, active usage, renewal, and continued preference.

## Build the approach
1. Identify meaningful lifecycle segments.
2. Understand reasons for disengagement or churn.
3. Design useful interventions around customer needs.
4. Set contact frequency and suppression rules.
5. Measure incremental retention and customer experience.
