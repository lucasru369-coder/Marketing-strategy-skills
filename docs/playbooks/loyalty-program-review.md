# Loyalty Program Review

## Review questions
Does the program reward behavior that matters to customers and the business? Is value understandable, attainable, sustainable, and fair?

## Dimensions
Value proposition, earning rules, redemption friction, breakage, liability, engagement, incremental behavior, fraud controls, and accessibility.

## Scorecard
| Dimension | Current evidence | Customer impact | Commercial impact | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not equate points issued with loyalty created. Evaluate incremental behavior and total program economics.
