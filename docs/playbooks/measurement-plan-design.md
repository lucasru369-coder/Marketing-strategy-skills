# Measurement Plan Design

## Start with decisions
Define what decision the measurement will support and who will use the result.

## Plan components
1. Objective and hypothesis.
2. Primary outcome metric and guardrails.
3. Baseline, target, and measurement window.
4. Data source and event definitions.
5. Attribution or comparison method.
6. Reporting cadence and owner.
7. Known limitations and privacy controls.
