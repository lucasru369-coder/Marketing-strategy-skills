# Marketing Knowledge Management

## What to preserve
Decision rationale, reusable methods, approved templates, metric definitions, research sources, experiment learnings, and operational guidance.

## Knowledge lifecycle
Create → review → approve → publish → maintain → archive.

## Metadata
Each resource should include purpose, owner, audience, version, last-reviewed date, and related resources.

## Maintenance rules
Set review intervals based on how quickly information changes. Remove obsolete guidance from active indexes while preserving history where required. Avoid storing personal data, credentials, confidential strategy, or copyrighted material without permission.
