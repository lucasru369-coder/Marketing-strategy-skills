# Brand and Performance Integration

## Shared planning
Align brand-building and activation around the same audience, proposition, customer journey, and business objective.

## Balance considerations
- Short-term conversion versus future demand
- Reach and mental availability versus immediate response
- Creative consistency versus format adaptation
- Brand metrics versus commercial outcomes

## Integrated plan
| Audience stage | Role | Message | Channel | KPI | Time horizon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Avoid assuming one universal budget ratio. Use category dynamics, buying cycle, evidence, and business stage to guide investment.
