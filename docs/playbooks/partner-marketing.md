# Partner Marketing

## Fit assessment
Evaluate audience overlap, complementary value, strategic fit, credibility, delivery capability, brand safety, and operational complexity.

## Partnership design
Define shared objective, audience, proposition, roles, contribution, budget, deliverables, approval rights, data responsibilities, and exit conditions.

## Partner plan
| Workstream | Partner role | Internal owner | Deliverable | Due date | Dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Agree measurement and lead/data handling before launch. Use written agreements for rights, claims, confidentiality, payment, and termination.
