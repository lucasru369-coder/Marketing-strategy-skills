# Marketing Automation Governance

## Governance areas
Purpose, audience eligibility, data source, consent, trigger logic, frequency caps, suppression, personalization, content approval, monitoring, and ownership.

## Before activation
Test entry and exit conditions, edge cases, time zones, duplicate prevention, unsubscribe behavior, and fallback content.

## Automation register
| Automation | Trigger | Audience | Data used | Owner | Review date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Review active automations periodically. Disable flows that are no longer relevant or whose data and content are outdated. Minimize personal data and apply access controls.
