# Email Marketing Quality

## Audience and permission
Use appropriate permission and lawful basis. Maintain suppression lists and honor unsubscribe requests promptly.

## Message quality
Subject and preheader should set accurate expectations. Keep one clear primary action, readable hierarchy, useful content, and mobile-friendly layout.

## Technical QA
Test rendering, links, tracking, sender identity, personalization fallbacks, accessibility, and deliverability configuration.

## Performance
Review delivery, bounce, complaint, unsubscribe, click, conversion, and downstream quality. Interpret open rates cautiously because privacy features can distort them.

Never use deceptive subject lines or unapproved personal-data enrichment.
