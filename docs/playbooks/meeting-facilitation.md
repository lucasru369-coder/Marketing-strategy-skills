# Meeting Facilitation

## Before the meeting
State the purpose, required decision, participants, pre-read, and timebox. Invite only people needed to decide, contribute expertise, or execute follow-up.

## During the meeting
1. Restate the desired outcome.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, and opinions.
3. Make space for dissent and quieter participants.
4. Keep discussion on the decision at hand.
5. Summarize options and trade-offs.
6. Confirm decision, owner, and due date.

## Decision log
| Topic | Options | Decision | Rationale | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Send concise notes promptly. If no decision is required, consider whether an asynchronous update is more efficient.
