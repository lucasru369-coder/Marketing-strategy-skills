# Product Launch Marketing

## Launch readiness
Confirm product availability, audience, positioning, proof, pricing, claims, support, channel readiness, inventory, and measurement.

## Launch phases
Pre-launch education and demand building; launch-day coordination; post-launch optimization and sustained storytelling.

## Launch review
Track awareness, qualified interest, conversion, availability, customer feedback, service issues, and channel execution. Separate launch communication performance from product or supply constraints.
