# Brand Safety Review

## Scope
Review placement context, creator/partner history, adjacent content, audience suitability, claims, data practices, and potential reputational exposure.

## Risk assessment
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Evidence | Mitigation | Decision owner |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

## Controls
Set category exclusions, placement controls, escalation triggers, monitoring cadence, and response responsibilities. Reassess when the context changes.

Avoid blanket assumptions about communities or cultures. Apply consistent, documented criteria and escalate legal or safety questions to qualified specialists.
