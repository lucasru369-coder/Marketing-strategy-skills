# Marketing Performance Diagnosis

## Diagnostic sequence
1. Validate tracking and reporting.
2. Compare against baseline, target, and relevant seasonality.
3. Locate the variance by funnel stage, market, audience, channel, or creative.
4. Separate volume, rate, and value effects.
5. Review operational changes and external factors.
6. Form testable explanations and prioritize actions.
