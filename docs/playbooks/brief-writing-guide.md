# Brief Writing Guide

## A useful brief answers
Why are we doing this? Who is it for? What should change? What is the single-minded message? What must be delivered? What constraints apply? Who decides?

## Recommended structure
Context, objective, audience, insight, proposition, proof, deliverables, channels, mandatories, exclusions, budget, timing, measurement, and approvers.

## Quality test
- Is the problem distinct from the requested output?
- Are audience and behavior specific?
- Can success be measured?
- Are mandatory and optional elements separated?
- Are dependencies and open questions visible?

A brief should enable good decisions without prescribing every execution detail.
