# Social Media Operations

## Operating model
Define channel purpose, audience, content owner, publishing access, moderation responsibility, escalation contact, and reporting cadence.

## Daily and weekly checks
Review scheduled posts, links, comments, community questions, platform notices, and emerging reputational risks. Escalate safety, privacy, legal, or crisis issues through approved routes.

## Workflow
Brief → draft → review → schedule → publish → moderate → report → learn.
