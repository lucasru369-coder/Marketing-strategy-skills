# Marketing Risk Escalation

## Classify
Assess severity, likelihood, urgency, affected audience, legal/safety implications, and reversibility.

## Escalation steps
1. Stop or contain activity when immediate harm is plausible.
2. Preserve relevant evidence securely.
3. Notify the designated accountable owner.
4. Involve legal, privacy, security, product, or communications specialists as appropriate.
5. Agree an action, spokesperson, and update cadence.
6. Record resolution and preventive learning.
