# Attribution Model Selection

## Common approaches
First-touch, last-touch, linear, position-based, time-decay, data-driven, and incrementality testing each answer different questions.

## Selection factors
- Buying cycle and number of touchpoints
- Data quality and identity limitations
- Channel mix and offline influence
- Decision being made
- Cost and analytical capability

## Model comparison
| Model | What it emphasizes | Strength | Blind spot | Appropriate use |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Attribution allocates observed credit; it does not automatically prove causality. Compare platform-reported results with independent measurement and experiments where feasible.
