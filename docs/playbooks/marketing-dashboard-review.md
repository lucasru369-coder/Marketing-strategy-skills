# Marketing Dashboard Review

## Before publishing
Confirm metric definitions, date ranges, filters, currency, time zone, attribution window, and source freshness.

## Dashboard structure
- Outcome metrics
- Leading indicators
- Channel and audience diagnostics
- Spend and efficiency
- Delivery or operational health
- Data-quality alerts

## Review questions
What changed? Compared with what? Why might it have changed? How confident are we? What action follows?

Avoid decorative metrics and overloaded dashboards. Every chart should support a question or decision. Document metric owners and refresh frequency.
