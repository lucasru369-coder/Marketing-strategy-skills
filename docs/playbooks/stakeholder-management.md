# Stakeholder Management

## Map stakeholders
Assess influence, interest, expertise, impact, and preferred involvement. Do not equate seniority with the only relevant perspective.

## Engagement plan
| Stakeholder/group | Interest | Influence | Needed input | Engagement method | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Practices
Clarify what is being consulted versus decided. Share context before requesting feedback. Surface disagreement early and record unresolved trade-offs. Tailor detail to the decision each stakeholder needs to make.

Review the map when scope, organizational context, or decision rights change.
