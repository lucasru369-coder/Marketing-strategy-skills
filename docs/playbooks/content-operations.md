# Content Operations

## Workflow
Intake → brief → prioritization → creation → review → approval → publishing → measurement → archive.

## Operating controls
Define content owner, editor, approver, channel requirements, asset naming, accessibility checks, rights clearance, localization, and version control.

## Editorial calendar
| Content | Audience need | Channel | Owner | Draft | Review | Publish | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Build realistic review buffers and capacity limits. Track recurring bottlenecks and improve the process rather than relying on last-minute escalation.
