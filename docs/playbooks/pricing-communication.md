# Pricing Communication

## Principles
Make the price, currency, billing period, eligibility, mandatory fees, and material conditions easy to understand.

## Review areas
- Reference price and discount basis
- Bundle contents and exclusions
- Subscription renewal and cancellation
- Taxes, shipping, and regional differences
- Availability and promotion end dates
- Required disclosures and local rules

## Price communication review
| Claim/display | Market | Evidence | Qualification | Reviewer | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Never create artificial urgency or imply a saving that cannot be substantiated. Refer legal interpretation to qualified counsel.
