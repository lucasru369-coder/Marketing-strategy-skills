# ROI and Incrementality

## ROI basics
Define the return and investment consistently. State whether return means revenue, gross profit, contribution margin, or another value.

## Incrementality
Ask what would have happened without the activity. Possible methods include randomized holdouts, matched markets, pre/post comparisons with controls, and marketing mix analysis.

## Evaluation
| Method | Data needs | Strength | Limitation | Best-fit question |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Include production, media, agency, technology, and operational costs where relevant. Avoid treating attributed revenue as incremental return. State uncertainty and the time horizon.
