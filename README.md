# Marketing Strategy Skills

A small collection of practical skills for marketing research and strategy development.

## Skills

- [Marketing Research](./marketing-research/SKILL.md): A structured workflow for market, audience, competitor, cultural, and localization research.

## Principles

- Use reliable, current sources and cite them.
- Separate verified facts, estimates, and hypotheses.
- Make assumptions explicit; never invent data.
- Turn research findings into clear, actionable recommendations.
- Keep outputs concise and adapted to the decision being made.
