# Market Entry Marketing Checklist

- [ ] Market demand and audience need reviewed
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapped
- [ ] Positioning and proof points validated
- [ ] Local channel mix selected
- [ ] Language and terminology reviewed
- [ ] Legal and claims requirements checked
- [ ] Pricing, currency, and offer mechanics localized
- [ ] Customer support readiness confirmed
- [ ] Measurement owners assigned
- [ ] Launch risks and contingency actions documented
