## Usage rules
- Include product, feature, campaign, and legal terminology.
- Add context where a term has multiple meanings.
- Mark terms that must remain untranslated.
- Record capitalization, spacing, and pluralization rules.
- Version the glossary and communicate changes.

Review terminology with market stakeholders and relevant product or legal owners. Avoid using machine-generated suggestions as final approval.
