# Localization QA Checklist

## Language
- Meaning matches the approved source intent.
- Grammar, spelling, punctuation, and terminology are reviewed.
- Tone is appropriate for the audience and channel.
- Dates, currencies, units, and address formats are correct.

## Design and functionality
- Text fits layouts and responsive states.
- Line breaks and reading order are natural.
- Links, QR codes, and forms work in-market.
- Fonts support required characters.
- Captions and accessibility labels are localized.

## Governance
- Claims and disclaimers are approved.
- Product names and trademarks follow naming rules.
- Final market reviewer and version are recorded.
