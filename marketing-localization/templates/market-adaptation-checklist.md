# Market Adaptation Checklist

Use this checklist when adapting a global campaign or brand asset for a local market.

## Strategy

- [ ] The local business objective is clear.
- [ ] The priority audience and local need are validated.
- [ ] The global proposition remains accurate and relevant.
- [ ] Local adaptations have a clear reason, not just a stylistic preference.

## Language and messaging

- [ ] Translation has been reviewed by a qualified local-language reviewer.
- [ ] Tone, idioms, humor, and wordplay are appropriate.
- [ ] Product names and technical claims are consistent.
- [ ] Dates, units, currencies, and address formats are localized.
- [ ] Text expansion and layout constraints have been checked.

## Culture and context

- [ ] Symbols, colors, gestures, and imagery have been reviewed in context.
- [ ] Local holidays and sensitive dates have been considered.
- [ ] Religious, political, and social sensitivities have been assessed.
- [ ] People and settings are represented respectfully and plausibly.

## Channels and execution

- [ ] Channel selection reflects local audience behavior.
- [ ] Formats and specifications match local platform requirements.
- [ ] Retail, partner, and distribution constraints are understood.
- [ ] Local legal, privacy, and promotion rules have been checked.

## Approval and measurement

- [ ] Local market owner has approved the adaptation.
- [ ] Product and legal claims have appropriate approval.
- [ ] Tracking and KPI definitions are consistent.
- [ ] Final assets and approval versions are archived.

Record exceptions, owners, and unresolved risks before release.
