# Market Prioritization

## Evaluation dimensions
Market potential, audience fit, competitive intensity, channel access, operational readiness, regulatory complexity, investment, and strategic learning value.

## Scorecard
| Market | Potential | Fit | Access | Readiness | Complexity | Investment | Weighted score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Define scoring anchors before rating markets. Use evidence confidence alongside the score and avoid false precision when data quality differs.

## Decision
Group markets into invest, validate, monitor, or defer. Document dependencies and the conditions that would change the ranking.
