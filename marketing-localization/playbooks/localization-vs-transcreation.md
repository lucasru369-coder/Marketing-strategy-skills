# Localization vs. Transcreation

## Choose localization when
Meaning is stable and the task is to adapt language, formats, units, conventions, and required market details.

## Choose transcreation when
The original concept depends on wordplay, cultural references, emotional nuance, humor, or a behavior that does not transfer directly.

## Decision guide

|---|---|---|---|

## Workflow
Preserve the communication objective, identify what must remain invariant, develop local alternatives, review with native-market expertise, and test comprehension.

Do not assume transcreation means unrestricted rewriting; maintain strategic intent and verify claims.
