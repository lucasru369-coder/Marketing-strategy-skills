# Market Entry Sequencing

## Purpose
Sequence market-entry work so that demand generation does not outrun product, channel, service, or compliance readiness.

## Workstreams
1. Market and customer validation.
2. Offer, pricing, and claims readiness.
3. Channel and partner setup.
4. Local content and campaign preparation.
5. Customer support and operational readiness.
6. Launch measurement and feedback.

## Readiness gates
| Gate | Evidence required | Owner | Go/no-go decision |
|---|---|---|---|

Define minimum readiness for each gate. A launch date should not override unresolved safety, legal, product availability, or customer-support blockers.
