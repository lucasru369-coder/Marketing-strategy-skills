# Translation Vendor Briefing

## Brief inputs
Source files, target locale, audience, purpose, channel, tone, terminology, product context, character limits, deadline, and review process.

## Reference package
Provide approved glossary, prior examples, brand voice guidance, visual context, and notes on untranslatable elements.

## Quality expectations
Agree on translator and reviewer roles, query handling, revision rounds, file format, and final QA. Specify whether the task is translation, transcreation, or linguistic review.

## Handover checklist
Confirm all assets, fonts, links, and contextual notes are included. Do not send personal or confidential source data unless authorized and necessary.
