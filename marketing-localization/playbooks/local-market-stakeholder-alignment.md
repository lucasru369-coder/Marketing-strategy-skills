# Local Market Stakeholder Alignment

## Before the meeting
Share the objective, decision needed, known constraints, and materials in the local working language where practical.

## Alignment sequence
1. Confirm the business outcome and audience.
2. Separate global non-negotiables from local choices.
3. Review market evidence and cultural considerations.
4. Assign decision rights and escalation routes.
5. Confirm owners, deadlines, and dependencies.

## Decision record
| Topic | Global principle | Local recommendation | Decision owner | Outcome | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Summarize decisions in writing. Avoid relying on implied agreement or informal chat history for critical approvals.
