# Localization Workflow

## Workflow stages
1. Receive a complete brief and source assets.
2. Confirm market, audience, channel, and legal constraints.
3. Separate fixed brand elements from adaptable content.
4. Translate and culturally adapt with qualified reviewers.
5. Check layout expansion, fonts, symbols, and device rendering.
6. Complete local legal and stakeholder review.
7. Archive approved assets and decisions.

## Release gate
Do not publish with unresolved language, rights, product, or compliance questions.
