# Market Readiness Assessment

## Dimensions
- Audience and demand evidence
- Product and service availability
- Language and content readiness
- Regulatory and claims review
- Channel and partner readiness
- Customer support and operations
- Measurement and data access

## Assessment
| Dimension | Status | Evidence | Gap | Risk | Owner | Required-by date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use clear status definitions: ready, ready with mitigation, blocked, or not assessed. Do not treat a translated campaign as proof that a market is operationally ready.
