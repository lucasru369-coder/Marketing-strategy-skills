# Initiative Dependency Map

| Initiative | Dependency | Type | Needed by | Dependency owner | Impact if late | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Dependency types
Decision, data, creative asset, product readiness, supplier, technology, legal approval, market input, or staffing.

## Review prompts
- Is the dependency confirmed or assumed?
- Does the owner have capacity and authority?
- Is there a fallback?
- Which milestone is affected?
- What needs escalation now?

Review critical dependencies at kickoff and each milestone. Record changes when scope or timing moves.
