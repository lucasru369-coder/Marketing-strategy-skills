# Marketing Decision Rights Matrix

| Decision area | Recommends | Approves | Consulted | Informed | Escalation route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Common decision areas
Strategy, audience, positioning, budget, creative direction, claims, market adaptation, channel mix, launch timing, and performance changes.

## Principles
Assign one accountable approver for each decision. Separate subject-matter review from approval authority. Define thresholds for decisions that can be made locally and those requiring central review.

Revisit the matrix when team structure, risk, or project scope changes.
