## Risk review prompts

- What assumptions could invalidate the plan?
- Which approvals or dependencies could delay delivery?
- What external changes could affect the market or audience?
- Where is capacity insufficient or overcommitted?
- What is the consequence of missing a key milestone?
- Which risks require a decision rather than monitoring?

Review high-impact risks at each planning checkpoint. Record changes in status and the owner responsible for the next action.
