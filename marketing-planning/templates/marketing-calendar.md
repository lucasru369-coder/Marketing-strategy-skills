## 8. Calendar quality checklist

- [ ] Every activity has a clear objective.
- [ ] Market, time zone, and dates are explicit.
- [ ] Fixed commitments are distinguished from ideas.
- [ ] Production lead times are included.
- [ ] Channel roles are defined.
- [ ] Owners and dependencies are visible.
- [ ] Overlaps and resource conflicts are reviewed.
- [ ] Dates and cultural moments are validated.
