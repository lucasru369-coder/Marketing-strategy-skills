## Decision quality checks

- Is the decision within the authority of the approver?
- Are the relevant options and trade-offs visible?
- Are assumptions and evidence separated?
- Is the impact on budget, scope, and timing understood?
- Is the next action assigned to a named role?
- Is there a trigger for revisiting the decision?

Use this log for material planning choices. Routine task updates belong in the project tracker.
