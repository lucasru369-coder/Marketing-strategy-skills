## 6. Core calculations

- Remaining approved budget = Approved budget − Actual spend.
- Forecast variance = Forecast total − Approved budget.
- Budget utilization = Actual spend ÷ Approved budget.
- ROAS = Attributed revenue ÷ Advertising spend.
- Marketing ROI = (Incremental contribution attributable to marketing − Marketing investment) ÷ Marketing investment.

Use these formulas only when input definitions are consistent. ROAS is not the same as incremental marketing ROI.
