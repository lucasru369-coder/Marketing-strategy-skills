# Marketing KPI Dictionary

A measurement reference for defining marketing indicators consistently across teams and markets.

## 1. KPI specification

Every KPI should have a documented definition.

| Field | Definition |
|---|---|
| KPI name | Consistent metric name |
| Objective | What decision or outcome it supports |
| Formula | Exact calculation |
| Numerator / denominator | Required components |
| Scope | Market, audience, product, channel |
| Data source | System or research source |
| Attribution | Attribution model and window, if applicable |
| Frequency | Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly |
| Owner | Person or team responsible for quality |
| Limitations | Known gaps, bias, or interpretation risks |

## 2. Common KPI groups

### Awareness

- Reach: Number of unique people exposed, subject to platform measurement definitions.
- Impressions: Total recorded exposures.
- Frequency: Impressions divided by reach.
- Brand awareness: Share of a defined research sample recognizing the brand.
- Share of search: Brand-related search interest relative to a defined competitive set and methodology.

### Engagement

- Engagement rate: Define the interaction numerator and exposure denominator explicitly.
- Video completion rate: Completed video views divided by the chosen eligible view count.
- Participation: Number of valid participants in a defined activity.
- Click-through rate: Clicks divided by impressions or another explicitly stated denominator.

### Consideration and demand

- Qualified traffic: Visits meeting documented quality criteria.
- Product-page engagement: Defined interactions with product content.
- Leads: Contacts meeting the agreed qualification definition.
- Lead conversion rate: Converted leads divided by the defined lead cohort.

### Conversion and efficiency

- Conversion rate: Conversions divided by eligible sessions, users, or clicks; specify which.
- Customer acquisition cost: Acquisition-related spend divided by new customers under a defined attribution approach.
- ROAS: Attributed revenue divided by advertising spend.
- Marketing ROI: Incremental contribution attributable to marketing, less marketing investment, divided by marketing investment.

### Retention

- Repeat purchase rate: Customers with more than one purchase divided by the defined customer cohort.
- Retention rate: Customers retained over a period divided by the eligible starting cohort, using a documented method.
- Customer lifetime value: Expected value over a defined relationship horizon and model.

## 3. Measurement cautions

- Platform-reported metrics may use different definitions.
- Reach across platforms may contain duplicate people.
- Attribution does not automatically establish incrementality.
- Revenue is not the same as profit or contribution margin.
- Correlation does not establish causation.
- Small samples may produce unstable rates.
- Cohort definitions and reporting windows must remain consistent.
- Changes in tracking can break comparisons with historical periods.

## 5. Measurement readiness checklist

- [ ] Objective and KPI are connected.
- [ ] Formula and denominator are documented.
- [ ] Data source is accessible and reliable.
- [ ] Baseline and reporting window are defined.
- [ ] Attribution limitations are stated.
- [ ] Owner and review cadence are assigned.
- [ ] Tracking is tested before launch.
- [ ] Privacy and consent requirements are respected.
