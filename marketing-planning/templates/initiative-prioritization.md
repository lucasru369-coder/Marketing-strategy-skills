Use this framework to compare competing initiatives when budget, time, or team capacity is limited.

Do not score initiatives until the decision context and constraints are clear.

## 2. Eligibility screen

An initiative should pass these checks before scoring:

- Is it legal and compliant?
- Is there a clear objective?
- Is there a defined audience or market?
- Is there a plausible delivery owner?
- Are critical dependencies understood?
- Is the cost estimate sufficiently credible?

If an initiative fails a mandatory requirement, resolve the gap or exclude it from the current portfolio.

## 3. Weighted scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Define what a low, medium, and high score mean before comparing initiatives.

| Dimension | Suggested weight | Score guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Strategic alignment | 25% | Contribution to approved strategic priorities |
| Expected business impact | 25% | Plausible scale of contribution to desired outcomes |
| Audience relevance | 15% | Strength of audience need and market fit |
| Timing | 10% | Importance of the opportunity window |
| Evidence confidence | 10% | Quality of evidence supporting expected impact |
| Feasibility | 15% | Delivery readiness, capacity, and dependency risk |
| Total | 100% | |

Weighted score = Sum of (dimension score × dimension weight).

This is a discussion aid, not an objective prediction of return. Adjust weights to reflect the organization's strategy.

## 5. Portfolio balance

Review whether the selected portfolio includes an appropriate mix of:

- Mandatory commitments
- Near-term demand capture
- Long-term brand and demand creation
- Customer retention
- Market development
- Experiments and learning
- Measurement and capability building

Avoid selecting only the highest-scoring initiatives if doing so creates excessive concentration in one market, channel, or time period.

## 8. Review questions

- Which initiatives are mandatory?
- Which initiatives have the strongest evidence?
- Which assumptions carry the most uncertainty?
- What is the opportunity cost of each selection?
- What will be stopped or delayed to make room?
- What conditions would trigger reprioritization?
