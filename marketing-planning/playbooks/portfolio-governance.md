# Portfolio Governance

## Purpose
Create a repeatable way to select, sequence, monitor, and stop marketing initiatives across teams.

## Governance cadence
- Monthly: delivery health, dependencies, and emerging risks.
- Quarterly: strategic fit, value evidence, capacity, and reprioritization.
- Annually: portfolio outcomes, capability gaps, and investment principles.

## Portfolio review
| Initiative | Strategic fit | Expected value | Confidence | Effort | Risk | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

## Decision options
Continue, accelerate, pause, rescope, combine, or stop. Record the rationale, owner, and next review date. A portfolio review should make trade-offs explicit rather than simply report status.
