# Initiative Portfolio Prioritization

Assess strategic alignment, audience impact, expected value, confidence, effort, dependencies, and risk. Balance quick wins with longer-term bets. Consider time horizon, market coverage, channel mix, and team capacity.

## Decision categories
- Commit: resourced and approved
- Test: small investment to reduce uncertainty
- Defer: valuable but not ready
- Stop: weak fit or evidence

Document rationale and revisit triggers.
