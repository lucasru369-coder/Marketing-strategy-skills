# Annual Budget Allocation

## Allocation principles
Start from strategic priorities and expected outcomes, not from last year's percentages alone. Protect essential operations while preserving room for learning and emerging opportunities.

## Process
1. Confirm business targets and strategic choices.
2. Estimate required investment by initiative and market.
3. Separate committed, baseline, growth, and experimental spend.
4. Model scenarios and resource constraints.
5. Set governance for release, reallocation, and review.

## Allocation view

|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

Document assumptions and trade-offs. Review allocations when evidence or business conditions materially change.
