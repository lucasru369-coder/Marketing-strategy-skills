# Marketing Scenario Planning

## Build scenarios
Choose two or three external uncertainties with material impact, then create plausible combinations. Scenarios are planning tools, not forecasts.

## For each scenario
- Market and audience conditions
- Demand and channel implications
- Cost, supply, and operational effects
- Opportunities and threats
- Strategic response and trigger points

## Scenario table
| Scenario | Key assumptions | Early indicators | Strategic response | No-regret actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Identify actions that work across scenarios and decisions that should wait for stronger signals. Assign owners to monitor indicators and define when the plan will be revisited.
