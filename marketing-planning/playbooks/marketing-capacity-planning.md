# Marketing Capacity Planning

## Purpose
Match planned work to available skills and time, including review, coordination, localization, and operational overhead.

## Workflow
1. List initiatives and required deliverables.
2. Estimate effort by role and phase.
3. Record availability, leave, and fixed responsibilities.
4. Identify bottlenecks and specialist dependencies.
5. Prioritize, sequence, outsource, or reduce scope.

## Capacity table

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|

Use realistic ranges rather than false precision. Reforecast when scope or timing changes. Do not assume that adding projects creates additional capacity.
