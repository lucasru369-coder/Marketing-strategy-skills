# Marketing Resource Planning

## Resource categories
People and skills, agency capacity, production suppliers, technology, data, budget, and market stakeholder time.

## Process
1. Translate priorities into resource demand.
2. Identify fixed and flexible capacity.
3. Compare demand with availability by period.
4. Surface specialist bottlenecks and critical dependencies.
5. Decide whether to sequence, simplify, train, or source externally.
