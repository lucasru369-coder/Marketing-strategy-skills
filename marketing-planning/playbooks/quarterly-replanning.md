# Quarterly Replanning

## Inputs
Current performance, market changes, initiative status, budget forecast, capacity, and updated assumptions.

## Review sequence
1. Compare outcomes with objectives.
2. Explain material variances.
3. Reassess assumptions and external conditions.
4. Review initiative value and delivery confidence.
5. Reallocate budget and capacity where justified.
6. Confirm revised targets, owners, and dates.

## Change record
| Decision | Evidence | Impact on scope/budget | Owner | Effective date |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Preserve the original plan for learning. Replanning should be evidence-led, not a routine reset of targets to match actual performance.
