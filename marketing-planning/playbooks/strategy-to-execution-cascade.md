# Strategy-to-Execution Cascade

## Cascade levels
Strategic choice → annual objective → quarterly outcome → initiative → deliverable → measure.

## Translation questions
- Which strategic choice does this objective support?
- What observable outcome should change?
- Which initiative can plausibly influence it?
- What must be delivered and by when?
- What evidence will show progress?

## Alignment table
| Strategy | Objective | Initiative | Deliverable | Measure | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Check vertical alignment and horizontal dependencies. If a deliverable cannot be connected to an outcome, reconsider its priority or explain its enabling role.
