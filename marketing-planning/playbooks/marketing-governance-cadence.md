# Marketing Governance Cadence

## Recommended forums
- Weekly delivery check: milestones, blockers, and immediate decisions.
- Monthly performance review: outcomes, spend, risks, and corrective actions.
- Quarterly portfolio review: strategic fit, investment, and capacity.
- Annual planning: priorities, assumptions, and capability needs.

## Meeting design
Every forum needs a clear purpose, decision rights, required inputs, time limit, and action log.

## Governance calendar

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cancel status meetings that have no decisions or coordination value. Share pre-reads early and record actions with owners and dates.
