# Campaign Budget Planning

## Build the estimate
List strategy, creative, production, media, talent, events, localization, technology, measurement, and contingency costs.

## Budget table
| Workstream | Quantity/assumption | Unit cost | Estimate | Confidence | Owner |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|

## Planning checks
- Separate committed from variable costs.
- Identify tax, agency, usage-rights, and shipping assumptions.
- Match spend timing to cash-flow and production milestones.
- Reserve contingency based on uncertainty.
- Define approval thresholds and reallocation rules.

Record currency, tax treatment, and estimate date. Do not hide uncosted dependencies in a general contingency line.
