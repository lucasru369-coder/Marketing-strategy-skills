# Campaign Optimization

## Diagnose before changing
Review delivery, audience quality, creative response, landing experience, conversion, and tracking integrity.

## Optimization loop
1. Confirm the metric is correctly implemented.
2. Compare performance with the agreed baseline and objective.
3. Segment results by meaningful audience, placement, or creative variables.
4. Form one testable explanation.
5. Change a limited number of variables.
6. Record the result and next decision.
