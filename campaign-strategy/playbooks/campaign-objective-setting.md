# Campaign Objective Setting

## Start with the business problem
Describe the change needed, the audience, and the reason a campaign is an appropriate intervention.

## Objective structure
For [audience], change [observable behavior or perception] from [baseline] to [target] by [date], measured through [metric].

## Check alignment
- Is the objective within campaign influence?
- Is the baseline reliable?
- Is the target ambitious but plausible?
- Is the measurement window appropriate?
- Are dependencies and non-campaign factors documented?

Separate business outcomes from communication outputs. Reach and impressions may support an objective but rarely define the full outcome.
