# Channel Role Definition

## Start with audience behavior
Identify where the audience discovers, evaluates, purchases, uses, and seeks support for the offer.

## Define each channel
- Role in the journey
- Audience and context
- Message and content format
- Desired action
- Primary metric and guardrail
- Cost and operational requirements
- Dependencies and limitations

## Channel map
| Channel | Journey role | Audience | Content | KPI | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not select channels solely because they are popular. Each channel should have a clear job and a realistic operating model.
