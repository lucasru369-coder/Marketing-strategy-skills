# Creative Platform Development

## Inputs
Audience tension, campaign objective, brand promise, product truth, cultural context, and channel behavior.

## Development
1. Write the audience insight in plain language.
2. Define the single-minded proposition.
3. Identify the reason to believe.
4. Explore several creative territories.
5. Test relevance, distinctiveness, flexibility, and brand fit.
6. Translate the selected territory into execution principles.

## Territory evaluation
| Territory | Audience relevance | Distinctiveness | Proof | Channel flexibility | Risks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

A creative platform is a repeatable idea system, not merely a tagline or visual style.
