# Content Pillar Development

## Inputs
Audience questions, brand expertise, product truth, search and social behavior, business objectives, and available production capacity.

## Process
1. Group audience needs into themes.
2. Define the role each theme plays.
3. Set proof and editorial boundaries.
4. Map formats and channels.
5. Create a sustainable publishing rhythm.
6. Review performance and audience feedback.

## Pillar matrix
| Pillar | Audience need | Brand contribution | Example topics | Format | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Avoid pillars that are only product categories or broad buzzwords. Each pillar should offer a repeatable reason for the audience to engage.
