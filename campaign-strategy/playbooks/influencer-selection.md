# Influencer Selection

## Evaluate fit
Audience overlap, content relevance, credibility, engagement quality, brand safety, production capability, usage rights, and cost.

## Due diligence
Review recent content, disclosure practices, audience authenticity indicators, past partnerships, category conflicts, and reputational risks. Use lawful, proportionate checks.

## Selection scorecard
| Candidate | Audience fit | Content fit | Credibility | Risk | Deliverables | Cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|

Define deliverables, disclosure, review rights, usage term, paid amplification, exclusivity, and measurement before contracting. Follower count alone is not a quality measure.
