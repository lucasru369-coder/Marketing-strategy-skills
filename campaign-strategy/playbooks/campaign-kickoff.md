# Campaign Kickoff Playbook

## Required inputs
Approved brief, objective, audience, proposition, scope, budget, timeline, decision owners, and known constraints.

## Kickoff agenda
1. Reconfirm the problem and desired outcome.
2. Review deliverables and out-of-scope items.
3. Walk through milestones and dependencies.
4. Agree review rounds and feedback format.
5. Confirm approval authority and escalation path.
6. Identify risks and immediate actions.

## Action log
| Action | Owner | Due date | Dependency | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

End with a shared written record. If key inputs are unresolved, label them as open decisions rather than silently treating assumptions as approved.
