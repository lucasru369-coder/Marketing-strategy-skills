# Event Marketing Planning

## Event purpose
Define whether the event is intended to generate awareness, qualified leads, trial, community, partner engagement, or sales.

## Planning stages
Pre-event audience building; on-site experience and capture; post-event follow-up and conversion.

## Operational checklist
Venue, audience flow, staffing, accessibility, power/network, production, permits, inventory, safety, lead consent, contingency, and measurement.

## Event plan
| Workstream | Deliverable | Owner | Deadline | Dependency | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Measure quality and follow-up, not footfall alone. Confirm permissions before collecting attendee data or publishing identifiable images.
