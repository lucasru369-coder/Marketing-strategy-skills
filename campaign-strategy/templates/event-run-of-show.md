## Pre-opening checks
Venue access, safety briefing, equipment, signage, stock, connectivity, accessibility, consent notices, and emergency contacts.

## Closeout
Reconcile materials and leads, secure data, capture issues, confirm supplier handback, and schedule follow-up.

Keep one controlled version available to the event team. Changes to timing or responsibilities should be communicated to all affected owners.
