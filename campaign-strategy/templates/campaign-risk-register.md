# Campaign Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood (1–5) | Impact (1–5) | Early warning | Mitigation | Contingency | Owner |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|

## Risk categories
Timing, production, supplier, budget, platform, audience response, reputation, cultural sensitivity, legal claims, data/privacy, and operational readiness.

Review high-impact risks at every major milestone. Assign one accountable owner per risk and specify the trigger that activates the contingency plan.
