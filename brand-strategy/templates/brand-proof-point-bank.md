# Brand Proof Point Bank

| Promise or message | Proof point | Evidence/source | Market applicability | Approved wording | Expiry/review date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Evidence types
Product specifications, independently verified results, service commitments, customer research, certifications, and documented organizational practices.

## Governance
Assign an owner to each claim. Confirm scope, market, and date before use. Flag claims requiring legal, regulatory, or technical review. Remove outdated proof rather than allowing old claims to circulate.

A proof point should substantiate the message, not merely repeat it in different words.
