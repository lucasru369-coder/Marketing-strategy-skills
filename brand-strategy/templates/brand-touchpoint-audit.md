# Brand Touchpoint Audit

| Touchpoint | Audience/context | Brand objective | Current experience | Consistency gap | Audience impact | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Coverage
Consider discovery, evaluation, purchase, onboarding, product use, service, community, and renewal.

## Review prompts
- Is the brand promise visible and credible here?
- Does the touchpoint help the audience complete its task?
- Is language and visual identity coherent?
- Are local requirements respected?
- Who owns the experience and its maintenance?

Sample both high-volume and high-friction touchpoints. Avoid judging consistency from visual appearance alone.
