# Brand Architecture Decision Guide

## Common models
- Branded house: one master brand across offers.
- House of brands: distinct brands with limited visible linkage.
- Endorsed brands: sub-brands supported by a parent.
- Hybrid: a deliberate combination.

## Decision factors
Audience overlap, portfolio complexity, equity transfer, acquisition strategy, channel needs, and governance cost.

## Evaluation
Map current and planned offers, clarify naming logic, and test whether customers understand the relationships. Choose the simplest system that supports the portfolio strategy.
