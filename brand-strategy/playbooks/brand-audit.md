# Brand Audit Playbook

## Audit dimensions
1. Strategy: purpose, audience, positioning, and differentiation.
2. Identity: logo, typography, color, imagery, and motion.
3. Expression: voice, messaging, and campaign consistency.
4. Experience: product, service, digital, retail, and support.
5. Governance: ownership, guidelines, and asset access.

## Method
Review representative touchpoints across markets and channels. Score consistency and effectiveness separately.

## Output
Prioritized gaps, evidence, business impact, quick wins, and longer-term recommendations.
