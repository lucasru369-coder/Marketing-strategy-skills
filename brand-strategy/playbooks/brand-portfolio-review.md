# Brand Portfolio Review

## Objective
Clarify the role of each brand, sub-brand, product line, and endorsed offer in the portfolio.

## Review dimensions
1. Audience and need served.
2. Role in the customer decision.
3. Distinctiveness and overlap.
4. Contribution to growth, margin, or reach.
5. Operational complexity and governance cost.

## Portfolio map
| Brand/line | Audience | Strategic role | Value contribution | Overlap risk | Future action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Possible actions include invest, clarify, combine, reposition, maintain, or retire. Base decisions on customer understanding and business evidence, not internal ownership alone.
