# Positioning Workshop Guide

## Preparation
Bring audience evidence, category context, competitor set, and business constraints.

## Workshop sequence
1. Agree on the audience and need.
2. Define the competitive frame.
3. Identify meaningful points of difference.
4. Test credibility and proof.
5. Draft alternative positioning routes.
6. Evaluate distinctiveness, relevance, and ability to deliver.

## Decision record
Capture the chosen route, rejected alternatives, unresolved questions, and evidence required before launch.
