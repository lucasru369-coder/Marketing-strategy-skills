# Brand Equity Measurement

## Define the question
Clarify whether the study measures awareness, associations, perceived quality, preference, trust, or loyalty. Avoid treating these as interchangeable.

## Suggested measures
- Awareness: aided and unaided recall.
- Associations: attributes linked to the brand.
- Consideration: inclusion in the choice set.
- Preference: relative choice under a defined scenario.
- Trust: confidence in claims and delivery.
- Loyalty: repeat behavior and resistance to switching.
