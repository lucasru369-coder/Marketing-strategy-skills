# Brand Guidelines Audit

## Audit scope
Review identity, typography, color, imagery, motion, voice, accessibility, localization, and asset governance across representative touchpoints.

## Audit checklist
- Are core assets current and easy to find?
- Are rules specific enough to guide execution?
- Are exceptions documented?
- Do digital and physical applications remain consistent?
- Can local teams adapt content without breaking brand meaning?
- Are accessibility and legal requirements covered?
