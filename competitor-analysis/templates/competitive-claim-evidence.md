## Assessment
- Is the claim factual, comparative, subjective, or aspirational?
- Is supporting evidence visible and relevant?
- Are important qualifications omitted?
- Does the claim apply to all products or only a specific model/market?
- What would independently verify it?

Use public information responsibly. Preserve source links and dates. Do not reproduce extensive copyrighted copy or infer confidential competitor strategy.
