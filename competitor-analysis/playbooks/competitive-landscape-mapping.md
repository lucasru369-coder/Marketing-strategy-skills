# Competitive Landscape Mapping

## Define the arena
Specify customer need, use case, geography, price tier, and relevant substitutes. Include indirect alternatives when they solve the same job.

## Map dimensions
Choose two dimensions that reflect real customer trade-offs, such as price versus service depth or simplicity versus customization. Avoid axes that merely make a preferred brand look unique.

## Interpretation
Identify clusters, underserved needs, crowded claims, and possible substitutes. Validate the map with customer evidence and revisit it when category boundaries shift.
