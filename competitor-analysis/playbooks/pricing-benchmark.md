# Pricing Benchmark Playbook

## Normalize before comparing
Record currency, tax treatment, region, product configuration, billing period, discount status, and observation date.

## Compare
- List price and typical transaction price
- Entry, mid, and premium tiers
- Bundles and subscription components
- Promotions and channel-specific offers
- Warranty or service differences

## Analysis
Compare price against perceived value and target audience willingness to pay. Avoid comparing unlike configurations or temporary discounts as if they were standard prices.
