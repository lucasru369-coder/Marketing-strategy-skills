# Substitute Analysis

## Objective
Understand what customers do instead of choosing the category or brand, including non-consumption and manual workarounds.

## Steps
1. Define the customer job and context.
2. List direct competitors, adjacent solutions, manual alternatives, and doing nothing.
3. Compare total effort, cost, risk, convenience, and perceived outcome.
4. Identify switching triggers and reasons to stay with the current solution.
5. Assess which substitutes are improving fastest.
