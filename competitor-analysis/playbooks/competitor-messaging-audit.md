# Competitor Messaging Audit

## Collection
Capture public-facing headlines, product pages, campaign claims, proof points, calls to action, and audience cues. Record channel, date, geography, and URL.

## Audit dimensions
- Audience and problem addressed
- Main promise and supporting benefits
- Functional versus emotional language
- Differentiating claims and evidence
- Tone, vocabulary, and recurring motifs
- CTA and conversion path

## Comparison

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Quote only short excerpts needed for analysis. Do not assume that repeated messaging is effective; compare it with customer response or performance evidence where available.
