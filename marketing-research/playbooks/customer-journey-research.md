# Customer Journey Research Playbook

## Scope
Map one audience pursuing one goal in one context. Avoid attempting to describe every possible journey in a single diagram.

## Stages
Trigger, discovery, evaluation, decision, onboarding, use, support, and advocacy. Combine or rename stages when the actual behavior requires it.

## Research prompts
- What starts the journey?
- What does the audience need to know or do next?
- Which touchpoints increase or reduce confidence?
- Where does effort, uncertainty, or delay appear?
- What prompts abandonment, repeat use, or recommendation?

## Journey map
| Stage | Audience goal | Behavior | Touchpoint | Friction | Evidence | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Validate the map using more than one evidence source. Label assumptions clearly and prioritize friction by frequency, severity, and business relevance.
