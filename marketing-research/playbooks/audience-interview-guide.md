# Audience Interview Guide

## Objective
Understand behaviors, motivations, barriers, and decision criteria—not merely collect opinions about a concept.

## Suggested flow
1. Context: “Tell me about the last time you…”.
2. Behavior: ask what happened, in sequence.
3. Alternatives: explore what else was considered.
4. Friction: identify effort, uncertainty, and trade-offs.
5. Outcome: ask what success looked like.

## Interview practice
Use neutral prompts, one question at a time, and follow concrete examples. Do not lead participants toward a preferred answer.

## Synthesis
Separate direct quotes, observed behavior, interpretation, and open questions. Note sample limitations.
