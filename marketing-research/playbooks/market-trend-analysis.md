# Market Trend Analysis Playbook

## Purpose
Distinguish durable market shifts from short-lived noise and translate evidence into planning implications.

## Workflow
1. Define category, geography, audience, and time horizon.
2. Collect signals from customer research, search behavior, sales patterns, industry reporting, regulation, and channel changes.
3. Classify each signal as emerging, accelerating, stable, or declining.
4. Rate magnitude, confidence, duration, and affected segments.
5. Identify implications for demand, positioning, channels, and capabilities.

## Trend register
| Signal | Evidence/source | Direction | Confidence | Impact | Time horizon | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Quality checks
Separate observation from interpretation. Seek disconfirming evidence. Avoid treating correlation as causation. Record when a signal should be reviewed again.
