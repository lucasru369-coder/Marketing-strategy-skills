# Audience Segmentation Method

## Principles
Useful segments are meaningfully different, measurable, reachable, sufficiently stable, and actionable.

## Candidate bases
Needs, behaviors, use occasions, value sought, lifecycle stage, attitudes, and relevant firmographic or demographic attributes.

## Process
1. Define the decision segmentation must improve.
2. Select variables linked to needs or behavior.
3. Form candidate groups from evidence rather than stereotypes.
4. Assess size, distinctiveness, reachability, and stability.
5. Test whether segments require different propositions, channels, or experiences.

## Segment profile
| Segment | Need/job | Behavior | Barrier | Value sought | Reachable channels | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not create segments that cannot change a marketing decision. Avoid sensitive attributes unless there is a clear lawful and ethical basis.
