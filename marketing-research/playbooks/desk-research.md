# Desk Research Playbook

## Research sequence
1. Clarify the question and scope.
2. Search primary sources first: official statistics, filings, standards, and company materials.
3. Use reputable secondary sources for context.
4. Cross-check important claims with independent sources.
5. Log publication date, access date, methodology, and caveats.

## Source quality rubric
Assess authority, recency, transparency, relevance, and potential bias.

## Deliverable
Summarize what is known, what is estimated, what remains uncertain, and which decision each finding supports.
