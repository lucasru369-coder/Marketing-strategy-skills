# Secondary Source Evaluation

## Evaluate each source
1. Authority: who produced it and what expertise do they have?
2. Method: how were data collected and analyzed?
3. Recency: does the publication date fit the decision horizon?
4. Scope: do geography, category, and audience match the question?
5. Incentives: could the publisher benefit from a particular conclusion?
6. Traceability: can key claims be checked against underlying evidence?

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Prefer original datasets and primary research for consequential claims. Treat syndicated summaries as leads until their methodology is understood. Record access date for changing sources.
