# Qualitative Interview Synthesis

## Before synthesis
Review the research question, sample profile, interview guide, consent conditions, and limitations. Remove identifying details from working notes.

## Coding workflow
1. Read notes without forcing an early conclusion.
2. Tag recurring needs, triggers, workarounds, objections, and language.
3. Group related codes into themes.
4. Compare themes across relevant participant groups.
5. Search for contradictions and outlier cases.
6. Translate themes into implications and follow-up questions.

Do not convert a qualitative frequency into a population percentage. Use anonymized, permissioned quotations only. Distinguish participant wording from researcher interpretation.
