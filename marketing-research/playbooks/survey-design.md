# Survey Design Playbook

## Before drafting
Define the decision the survey should inform, the target population, sampling approach, and required precision.

## Question principles
- Ask one thing at a time.
- Use balanced, mutually exclusive answer choices where possible.
- Include “Other” or “Not sure” when appropriate.
- Avoid assumptions and emotionally loaded wording.
- Keep demographic questions relevant and optional where possible.

## Review
Pilot the survey with a small group. Check completion time, skipped questions, confusing terms, and response patterns.

## Reporting
Disclose sample size, field dates, recruitment method, and limitations. Do not imply representativeness without evidence.
