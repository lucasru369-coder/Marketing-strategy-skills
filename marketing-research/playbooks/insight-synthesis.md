# Insight Synthesis Framework

## From evidence to insight
- Evidence: what was observed or measured?
- Pattern: what repeats across sources or participants?
- Tension: what need conflicts with an existing behavior or constraint?
- Insight: what useful explanation connects the pattern and tension?
- Implication: what could the organization change?

## Validation
Check for counterexamples, alternative explanations, and sample bias. Keep evidence traceable to its source.

## Writing format
“Although [current behavior], [audience] struggles with [tension] because [evidence-backed reason], creating an opportunity to [implication].” Treat this as a prompt, not a formula.
