# Market Demand Estimation

## Choose an approach
Use top-down estimates when reliable category totals exist; use bottom-up estimates when customer counts, purchase frequency, and spend assumptions are more credible. Triangulate both when possible.

## Bottom-up model
Potential customers × realistic adoption rate × purchase frequency × average net revenue.

## Procedure
1. Define category boundaries and target geography.
2. Remove overlaps between audience groups.
3. Use explicit assumptions for eligibility, awareness, conversion, and repeat purchase.
4. Build conservative, base, and upside cases.
5. Compare against independent sources and operational capacity.

Report a range, not false precision. State exclusions, currency, period, and whether values represent revenue, units, or customers.
