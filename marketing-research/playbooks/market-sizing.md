# Market Sizing Playbook

## Purpose
Estimate the scale of an addressable market without presenting assumptions as facts.

## Workflow
1. Define the category, geography, audience, and time period.
2. Select a top-down, bottom-up, or triangulation approach.
3. Record each input, source, date, and confidence level.
4. Build low, base, and high scenarios.
5. Check that segments do not overlap.

## Output
- TAM: total category demand under the stated definition.
- SAM: demand reachable with the current offer and coverage.
- SOM: realistically obtainable demand within the planning horizon.

## Quality checks
State units, currency, period, exclusions, and assumptions. Avoid treating SOM as a guaranteed forecast.
