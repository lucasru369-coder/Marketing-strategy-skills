# Survey Quality Control

## Design checks
- Every question supports a stated research objective.
- Response options are mutually exclusive where appropriate and collectively sufficient.
- Scales have balanced, clearly labeled endpoints.
- Questions avoid double-barreled wording and leading assumptions.
- Screening and routing logic are tested.

## Fieldwork checks
Monitor completion rate, breakoff point, response duration, straight-lining, duplicate submissions, and unusual response patterns. Define exclusion rules before reviewing results.

## Reporting
Disclose sample source, field dates, sample size, weighting, exclusions, and limitations. Do not imply representativeness when the sampling method does not support it.
