# Insight Prioritization Matrix

Score each insight consistently; use scores as a discussion aid, not as a substitute for judgment.

| Insight | Audience impact (1–5) | Evidence confidence (1–5) | Strategic relevance (1–5) | Actionability (1–5) | Priority rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

## Review questions
- Is the insight supported by more than one observation or source?
- Does it explain a meaningful behavior or barrier?
- Is it distinct from an already-known fact?
- Can a team act on it within the planning horizon?
- What additional evidence would change its priority?

Record minority or contradictory insights separately when they may matter for underserved audiences or future scenarios.
