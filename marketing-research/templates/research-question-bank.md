# Research Question Bank

Use only questions relevant to the decision at hand.

## Category
- What alternatives solve the same job?
- Which needs are underserved?
- What changes demand over time?

## Audience
- What triggers the search for a solution?
- What creates hesitation?
- Which trade-offs matter most?

## Channel
- Where does discovery happen?
- Which touchpoints influence evaluation?
- Where does the journey break?

## Brand
- What associations are distinctive?
- Which claims are credible and meaningful?
- What evidence supports trust?

Record the question, evidence needed, source, owner, and decision affected.
