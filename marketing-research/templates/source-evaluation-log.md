## Evaluation criteria

Score each source against the following questions:

- **Authority:** Is the publisher qualified to make this claim?
- **Method:** Is the data collection or analytical method explained?
- **Recency:** Is the information current enough for the decision?
- **Relevance:** Does it apply to the market, audience, and period in scope?
- **Independence:** Is there a commercial or other incentive that may shape the claim?
- **Traceability:** Can another person locate and verify the original material?

## Evidence handling

- Prefer primary sources for regulations, official statistics, and company announcements.
- Use multiple independent sources for material market claims.
- Do not treat a search snippet or unattributed chart as sufficient evidence.
- Preserve the original wording for direct quotations and cite the source.
- Flag estimates, modeled data, and directional indicators explicitly.
