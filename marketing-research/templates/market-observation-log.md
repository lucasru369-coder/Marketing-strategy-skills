# Market Observation Log

Use this log to capture changes worth investigating before treating them as established trends.

| Date observed | Market/context | Observation | Source | Possible explanation | Confidence | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Guidance
Record what was observed before interpreting why it happened. Note whether the observation is isolated or repeated. Link to the original source where permitted and capture the publication date.

## Monthly review
- Which observations repeated?
- Which were disproven?
- What new research question emerged?
- Does any observation affect a current assumption or plan?

Do not store personal data or confidential customer information in a public repository.
